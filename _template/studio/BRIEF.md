# <Product> — design brief

The product source of truth for every builder and auditor. Write it before the design system. Keep it factual, because builders follow it literally.

## 1 · Product
What it is, who pays for it, and the one problem it solves. Two or three sentences.

## 2 · Market & language
- City / region, currency (code + decimals), phone format.
- Language: Arabic-first / English-first / both equal. Form of address in Arabic (masculine / feminine / plural), MSA or dialect, and where dialect is allowed (e.g. only inside WhatsApp messages).
- Date and time conventions (12/24-hour, Levantine or Gulf month names).

## 3 · Rules
The non-negotiable product rules every screen must obey. Number them so the audit can cite them. Examples:
1. Who may see which money figures.
2. What can never be deleted (void / correct with a reason instead).
3. Privacy: what is shown to whom.
4. What red is reserved for.

## 4 · Roles
| Role | Device | Does | Never sees |
|---|---|---|---|

## 5 · Features
Per role, the jobs to be done, in the order of a real day. Mark launch scope (v1) and later phases (v2).

## 6 · Glossary

## 7 · Brand
Logo, palette anchors (or "derive from logo"), fonts, tone of voice, reference apps. Link `brand-spec.md` if it exists.

## 8 · Out of scope
What the boards must NOT show.
